# Brief

A silent, seamless motion banner for Melvin's GitHub profile. Use the supplied North Sea illustration and the typography and greens of his portfolio. Name is visible in the first frame. The moving sea and a gull make a personal loop rather than a product ad; the name stands alone without a subtitle. 1280x480 at 60 fps, 8 seconds, 120 BPM, calm, silent. GitHub receives an optimised looping GIF, with MP4 and WebM masters.

The supplied brand explicitly overrides Cinetic's generic ban on serif type and warm paper colours: Newsreader, DM Sans, ivory and forest green already belong to the portfolio. The supplied gull and wave imagery belong to this profile. No random filler, claims, fake interface, neon, sound, dot-separated text, or invented metrics.
